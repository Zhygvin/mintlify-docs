## Brand voice guidelines

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Technical but accessible" icon="code">
    We explain DSS mechanics precisely without assuming a cryptography PhD. Use analogies when they add clarity. Avoid hype words like "revolutionary" unless quoting a third party.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Specific over vague" icon="bullseye">
    Say "0.00018 kWh per handshake" instead of "low energy." Say "1 ms latency" instead of "fast." Numbers earn trust.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Verified not aspirational" icon="shield-check">
    Every capability we claim is backed by a production attestation. Do not describe future targets as current facts. Point to the telemetry report when in doubt.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Mission-driven" icon="rocket">
    NEOXZ exists to eliminate credentials, not to sell another layer of security. Frame stories around what disappears (passwords, seed phrases, friction) rather than what gets added.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Topic ideas for coverage

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="The end of passwords" icon="lock-open">
    Passwords have been declared dead for two decades but never buried. DSS is the first protocol that removes them architecturally rather than patching around them. Perfect for enterprise security and policy desks.

  <Card title="How to kill seed phrases" icon="fire">
    Self-custody crypto wallets rely on 12-word phrases that users lose, screenshot, or store in clouds. DSS replaces the backup with continuous device binding. A post-FTX custody story that does not require trusting a custodian.
  </Card>
