NEOXZ Engineering is raising capital to scale the Discrete Sovereign Settlement (DSS) protocol globally. DSS is a continuous authentication protocol built on triangulated reality-anchored handshakes, and it is already live in production with verified telemetry at [NEOXZ DSS Proof of Work](/dss/proof-of-work). The product has a Sovereign Tier A+ rating, 9.8 out of 10 on the Global Sovereign Net, and 1 millisecond latency. These are not projected or testnet numbers. They are production-verified metrics measured in the asia-southeast1 Cloud Run deployment.
