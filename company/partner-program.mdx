## Referral partner

The fastest path to partner revenue. No contracts, no quotas, just results.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Apply online">
    Submit the partner application and tell us your audience. Approval takes 2 business days.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive your tracking link">
    NEOXZ generates a unique UTM-tagged link and provides brand-safe assets you can drop into your content.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refer prospects">
    Share the link via email newsletters, blog posts, social threads, or conference intros. We track every touch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get paid monthly">
    When a deal closes within 90 days of your referral, you earn 20% of the contract value for the first 12 months. Payments land by the 15th of each month.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Reseller partner

For partners who want to own the full sales cycle and build a recurring services practice around DSS.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Apply and sign the reseller agreement">
    Commit to a $25K annual booking target. NEOXZ provides a standardized agreement with clear deal-registration rules.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete sales certification">
    Attend a 2-hour live workshop covering DSS value props, pricing, competitive positioning, and objection handling.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register deals">
    Log opportunities in the partner portal. Protected exclusivity for 90 days on registered accounts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close and grow">
    Earn 30% of every contract. Quarterly business reviews help you expand into multi-year enterpriseSSAs.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**What we provide:**

- Sales training and demo coaching
- Deal registration with exclusivity
- Co-branded pitch decks, one-pagers, and case studies
- Quarterly business reviews with NEOXZ leadership
- Pipeline visibility through a shared Slack channel
