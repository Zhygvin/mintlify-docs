The device authentication vector validates physical possession of the registered hardware device. It carries the highest weight in the composite trust score (40%) because Secure Enclave keys are effectively unextractable.

## Validation Mechanisms

### Secure Enclave Signature Verification

The gateway maintains a cached copy of each device's public key. Every handshake includes an ECDSA signature generated by the Secure Enclave private key. Validation confirms:

- The signature was generated by the specific registered device.
- The private key has not been cloned or extracted.
- The payload has not been tampered with in transit.

### Hardware Attestation Refresh

Every 100 handshakes (approximately 5 minutes), the device attaches a fresh hardware attestation proof. The attestation confirms:

- OS remains uncompromised (no jailbreak, root, or malware).
- Secure Enclave integrity is intact.
- Mock location services are disabled.
- No debugging or hooking frameworks are active.

<Warning>
  Failed attestation immediately reduces the device authentication score to `0.0`, blocking authentication regardless of the other vectors.
</Warning>

### Device Fingerprint Consistency

Each handshake includes a compressed device fingerprint (model, serial number hash, screen dimensions, sensor capabilities, installed secure elements). The gateway compares it against the baseline captured at registration. Fingerprint changes indicate device replacement or emulator use and trigger step-up authentication.

### Biometric Binding Validation

The biometric itself is never transmitted. The handshake includes the timestamp of the most recent successful biometric authentication. If this timestamp exceeds 8 hours, the device score is reduced by 30% and high-risk operations require a fresh biometric.

## Scoring

The device authentication score is the **minimum** of the four component checks so any single failure prevents authentication:

| Component | 1.0 | 0.9 | 0.7 | 0.5 | 0.0 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Signature validity | Valid | — | — | — | Invalid |
| Attestation | Passed | — | Stale (interpolated) | — | Failed |
| Fingerprint | Matches | — | Minor variance | — | Major mismatch |
| Biometric recency | \< 1 hour | \< 4 hours | \< 8 hours | \> 8 hours | — |

The result feeds into the [composite trust score](/dss/trust-score) with a 40% weight.
