NEOXZ Engineering partners with resellers, systems integrators, developer advocates, and OEMs to bring Discrete Sovereign Settlement (DSS) continuous authentication to every market. Every tier includes access to the DSS Partner Portal hosted in our Confluence workspace, plus co-marketing resources, technical enablement, and a clear path to revenue. Whether you make warm intros or embed DSS inside your own product, this program is built to move fast and pay fairly.

## Deal Registration

Registration protects your opportunity and locks in your commission rate.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Submit the opportunity">
    Email [partners@neoxz.engineering](mailto:partners@neoxz.engineering) with company name, decision maker, timeline, and estimated deal size.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive confirmation">
    NEOXZ confirms registration within **1 business day** and assigns a partner manager if applicable.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Exclusivity window">
    You receive **90-day exclusivity** on the opportunity. No channel conflict, no double-teaming.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get paid">
    Commission is paid within **30 days** of customer invoice.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Certification Program

Earn the **Certified DSS Integrator** credential by completing an 8-hour certification course plus a practical exam. Certified integrators are listed in our public partner directory and receive priority support and joint-content opportunities.

The course covers DSS architecture, deployment patterns, [SDK integration](/solutions/sdk-quickstart), and [compliance attestation](/solutions/compliance-attestation). Study materials and the exam are hosted in the [DSS Partner Portal](https://zhygvin.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/NeoXZ).
