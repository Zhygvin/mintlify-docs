## Why Now

Three industry shifts make this the right moment for DSS infrastructure.

**1. AI-driven phishing has made passwords unviable**

Large language models and voice synthesis engines now bypass traditional MFA at scale. Static credentials cannot adapt to real-time threat environments. DSS is credentialless by design: no password, no OTP, no seed phrase, nothing to phish.

**2. The SEC is pushing continuous controls monitoring**

Public companies and auditors face pressure to replace point-in-time snapshots with always-on compliance evidence. DSS produces tamper-resistant, timestamped audit trails as a native byproduct of every authentication event.

**3. Crypto adoption is blocked by seed phrase UX**

Self-custody growth stalls when users fear permanent asset loss. DSS removes seed phrases from the onboarding flow entirely, replacing them with social-recovery shards bound to real-world identity vectors.
