## Reference Architecture

A typical enterprise deployment integrates five layers:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Identity enrollment">
    Users enroll via your existing SSO or IDP. Biometric and Secure Enclave bonding happens inside your perimeter during onboarding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Continuous handshake layer">
    DSS gateway deployed next to your application tier. Validates handshakes every 3 seconds without round-tripping to your identity provider.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Trust score decisioning">
    Composite score (Device 0.40 + Contact 0.30 + Location 0.30) drives access control, step-up, and session termination inside your app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attestation and audit">
    All handshake outcomes are hashed, sealed, and streamed to your SIEM. Quarterly SEC-grade reports generated automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Recovery and continuity">
    Multi-device recovery via Shamir Secret Sharing. Lost-device scenarios resolve without password resets or support tickets.
  </Step>
</Steps>
