NEOXZ Authentication Carbon Credits convert the Discrete Sovereign Settlement (DSS) protocol's measured 92.1% energy reduction into verified, auditable carbon offsets. Enterprises buy credits to retire against Scope 2 and Scope 3 emissions, backed by cryptographically sealed telemetry.

## The Energy Problem We Solve

Global authentication infrastructure consumes enormous energy that most ESG programs do not even count:

- SMS OTP gateways route billions of messages per day across cellular backbones
- Email verification servers run 24/7 processing bounce-backs and retries
- Password reset flows trigger database writes, email delivery, and support tickets
- MFA app push notifications wake radios on billions of devices every day

The combined footprint is substantial, growing with every new user, and almost entirely replaceable.

## How Credits Are Generated

<Steps>
  <Step title="Baseline measurement">
    Enterprise reports current authentication volume and method (SMS, email, TOTP, push). We model baseline kWh using published grid intensities.
  </Step>
  <Step title="DSS deployment">
    Enterprise deploys DSS. Every handshake is logged with its cryptographic seal and timestamp.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delta calculation">
    We compute kWh saved per handshake (baseline minus DSS actual) using the operator's cloud region grid factor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Third-party verification">
    An accredited verifier audits the telemetry seal, grid factor, and delta calculation. Issues a verified carbon credit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Registry retirement">
    Credits are registered on a recognized carbon registry and retired against the buyer's emissions reporting.
  </Step>
</Steps>
