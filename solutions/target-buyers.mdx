NEOXZ DSS fits best where authentication fraud is expensive, compliance is adversarial, user friction kills conversion, or auth energy costs are material. This page maps the buyers, investors, and strategic partners we serve, and it doubles as a qualification reference for the sales team and Workato Genie.

## Primary Buyer Segments

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Fintech & Neobanks" icon="building-columns">
    Stripe, Plaid, Chime, Mercury, Brex, Ramp, Wise, Revolut, Nubank.

    **Pain:** SMS OTP fraud, account takeover, regulatory audit cost.

    DSS replaces SMS OTP and password resets with continuous, triangulated authentication (device, contact, location) at 1 ms latency and [Sovereign Tier A+ (9.8/10) verification](/dss/proof-of-work).
  </Card>

  <Card title="Crypto Exchanges & Wallets" icon="bitcoin-sign">
    Coinbase, Kraken, MetaMask (Consensys), Phantom, Rainbow, Ledger, Trezor, Fireblocks, BitGo.

    **Pain:** Seed phrase loss, support burden, phishing.

    DSS eliminates seed phrases via [sovereign wallet recovery](/solutions/wallet-recovery) while preserving self-custody, cutting support tickets and phishing exposure.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Enterprise SaaS" icon="building">
    Okta, Auth0, Workday, ServiceNow, Salesforce.

    **Pain:** MFA fatigue, SSO extension, insider threat.

    DSS integrates into existing IAM stacks, replacing push-notification fatigue with silent, continuous proof-of-presence. See the [SDK quickstart](/solutions/sdk-quickstart) for a weekend migration path.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Regulated Industries" icon="shield-halved">
    Healthcare systems (Epic, Cerner integrators), insurers (progressive carriers), defense contractors.

    **Pain:** HIPAA/CJIS/CMMC audit prep.

    DSS streamlines audit readiness with [SEC-grade compliance attestation](/solutions/compliance-attestation) and verifiable [proof-of-work](/dss/proof-of-work).
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Qualification Criteria

A qualified prospect for NEOXZ DSS meets at least one of the following thresholds:

- More than 100,000 monthly active users (MAU)
- More than $10,000 per month in authentication spend (SMS OTP, password reset, MFA licensing, or fraud chargebacks)
- Regulated industry status (healthcare, financial services, government/defense)
- Crypto-adjacent product (wallet, exchange, custody, or on-ramp)
- Scope 2 or greater ESG commitment with published carbon targets

<Note>
Prospects that meet two or more criteria are flagged as Priority Tier and fast-tracked to a solution architect call.
</Note>
